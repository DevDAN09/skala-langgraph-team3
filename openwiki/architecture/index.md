# Files

- [Evaluation Graph and Runtime Orchestration](orchestration.md) - The LangGraph StateGraph coordinates six evaluation nodes, beginning with parallel paper and market research and chaining market context into stakeholder research. Covers fallback behavior, source enrichment, and the no-winner invariant.
